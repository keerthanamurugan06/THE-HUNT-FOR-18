# ROUND 3 – BLIND CODE

## Overview
Round 3 is a partner-based investigation challenge.

**Provisional duration:** 18 minutes  
**Status:** Not finalized.

## Story Purpose
Continue the investigation and reveal additional information about the 18 victims.

The round must contribute meaningfully to the same storyline established in Rounds 1 and 2.

## Design Constraints
- Two participants per team.
- One laptop/device per team.
- The challenge must encourage cooperation.
- It must be engaging rather than repetitive.
- It must contribute to victim-related investigation progress.
- It must fit the organizer-controlled round duration.

## Previous Concept
The original concept involved one participant seeing a clue and another participant wearing a blindfold to select letters from a physical alphabet board.

The project owner felt this could become boring or repetitive and wanted to reconsider how the blindfold element is used.

## Alternatives Discussed – Not Approved
- Blind Navigator.
- Voice of Evidence.
- Memory Blackout.
- Silent Investigation.
- Unknown Object.
- The Switch Protocol.

These are brainstorming options only. None is approved for implementation.

## Pending Decisions
- Whether to retain the blindfold.
- Final two-person interaction.
- Physical versus digital components.
- Clue and answer format.
- Victim-related output.
- Scoring and penalties.
- Accessibility and safety arrangements.

Do not implement this round until the project owner approves its gameplay.
